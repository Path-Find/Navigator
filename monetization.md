# Navigator Monetization Plan

Navigator already has the right foundation for paid conversion: clear tiering, usage limits, Stripe plumbing, and a strong “analysis-first” value moment. The plan below focuses on activating revenue quickly, then expanding ARPU without diluting the core experience.

## Objectives
1. Convert trial users at the moment of maximum perceived value.
2. Grow ARPU with modular add-ons while keeping entry friction low.
3. Build retention loops so paid users stay through a full job cycle.

## Core Revenue Model
**Primary:** Subscription (Explorer → Plus → Pro) with monthly + annual billing.  
**Secondary:** Add-on credits and feature packs.  
**Tertiary:** B2B/campus partnerships.

## Packaging Strategy (current structure + refinements)

| Tier | Price | Audience | Value Anchor | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Explorer | Free | New users, skeptics | “See your true match score” | 3 lifetime analyses, limited alerts |
| Plus | $19/mo | Active seekers | “High-velocity job matching” | Weekly analysis cap, daily alert cap, mentor cap |
| Pro | $29/mo | Career pivots, high-stakes | “Unlimited intelligence loop” | Unlimited analyses, advanced cover letters, pro interview tools |

**Refinement:** Keep Explorer focused on *diagnosis* and lock *resolution* (tailoring, quality loop, interview depth) behind Plus/Pro.

## Trial & Paywall Conversion
1. **Curated 3‑job journey:** sequence the trial to show a “before → after” impact, then end on a clear gap that only paid tools solve.
2. **Personalized upgrade modal:** show their average match score and concrete deltas (“+18% fit if you close these 3 gaps”).
3. **Feature walling, not data walling:** let users see insights, lock actions (rewrite, export, interview prep).
4. **Urgency flows:** a high-intent “Interview Eve” mode that unlocks a short paid window or Pro trial.

## Add‑Ons & Packs (ARPU expansion)
1. **Job Pack**: Extra analyses or alert volume for a fixed monthly add‑on.
2. **Coach Pack**: Advanced gap analysis, role models, interview loops.
3. **Edu Pack**: Transcript analysis and academic benchmarking for students.
4. **Credit bundles**: One‑time purchases for bursts (e.g., 10 analyses).

## Channel Strategy
1. **Product-led**: free trial + viral referral (“give one, get one analysis”).
2. **Content/SEO**: landing pages for “ATS score,” “career pivot,” “interview prep.”
3. **Partnerships**: bootcamps, universities, alumni offices.
4. **Recruiter pipeline** (later): offer anonymized fit insights as a premium channel.

## Retention Loop
1. Weekly digest: pipeline status + top matches.
2. Bot nudges: interview prep drip, deadlines, and match alerts.
3. Outcome tracking: “applications → interviews → offers” progress reporting.

## Operational Plan (activation order)
1. **Turn on Stripe checkout** (remove waitlist gating when ready).
2. **Implement curated 3‑job trial flow** with progression messaging.
3. **Upgrade modal personalization** (average score + gap summary).
4. **Launch referral credits** and track attribution.
5. **Introduce add‑on packs** after baseline subscription conversion stabilizes.

## Metrics to Track
1. **Trial → paid conversion** (overall and per cohort).
2. **Activation**: % who complete 3 analyses within first 7 sessions.
3. **Paywall CTR** and **checkout completion**.
4. **ARPU** and **retention** (monthly churn).
5. **Feature adoption**: tailoring, cover letter loop, interview tools.

## Risks & Guardrails
1. **Too much free value** → keep action tools paid.
2. **Over‑fragmented pricing** → start with 3 tiers, add packs later.
3. **Trust concerns** → transparent usage limits and privacy positioning.
