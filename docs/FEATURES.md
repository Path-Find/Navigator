# Features

The Navigator platform is a suite of AI-powered tools designed to automate the manual labor of the job search process.

## Core Modules

### 1. Job Alerts
- **Email Ingestion (Active R&D)**: Forward your job alert emails (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.) to a unique Navigator address for background triage, de-duplication, and scoring.
- **Browser Extension**: Save jobs directly from major portals with a single click.
- **Smart Scraper**: Automated extraction of job metadata, bypassing site noise and boilerplate.

### 2. Match & Analysis (The Engine)
- **Instant Compatibility**: Real-time scoring (0-100) of how well your background aligns with a specific job description.
- **Gap Analysis**: Detailed breakdown of missing skills, experience overlaps, and potential "blind spots" in your profile.
- **Professional Insight**: AI-generated reasoning that explains *why* you are a fit (or why not).

### 3. Modular Resume & Drafting (The Output)
- **Resume Blocks**: Transform your experience into a modular library of "blocks" that can be hyper-tailored for every role.
- **AI Cover Letters**: Role-specific, grounded cover letters drafted using your unique narrative context.
- **Hyper-Tailoring**: Sequential AI pass that rewrites specific resume bullets to align with the target job's nomenclature.

### 4. Interview Companion (The Future)
- **Telegram Bot (Conceptual)**: Real-time alerts and conversational interview prep via messaging apps.
- **Interview Mock Sessions**: Guided practice sessions using role-specific AI prompts to simulate high-stakes interviews.
