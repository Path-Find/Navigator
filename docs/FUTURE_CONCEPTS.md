# Future Concepts (Stashed)

This document contains ideas, long-term vision items, and feature specs for Career Growth, Coaching, and Education modules that were stashed during the Jobs-first pivot. These can be restored or referenced if we expand the platform's scope in the future.

## Vision & Intelligence

- **Predictive Trajectory (Salary & Career)**: ML-based forecasting of salary levels, promotion timelines, and title path-modeling over 5–10 years.
- **Global Mobility Engine**: Automating visa eligibility checks, international relocation pathways, and cost-of-living adjustments for global career pivots.
- **Autonomous Job Agent**: Navigator automatically applying to high-match jobs on behalf of the user, managing the application funnel until an interview is booked.
- **High-Fidelity Prep**: Low-latency voice and video simulation for mock interview practice.

## Monetization & Pricing

- **Specialized Product Packs**: Offering modular, optional add-on "Packs" for **Coach** and **Edu** on top of the base Jobs subscription (e.g., career growth tracking, academic planning).

## Onboarding & Journeys

- **Career Growth Track**: Journey path tailored for users looking to grow in their current roles, level up, build new skills, or get promoted.
- **Academic / Student Track**: Journey path for managing studies, transcripts, course requirements, and analyzing graduate school admissions eligibility (e.g., Master's program matching).
