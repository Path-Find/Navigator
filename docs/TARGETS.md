# Targets

Performance and quality benchmarks for the Navigator platform.

## Performance
- **Initial Load**: < 2.0s for the primary dashboard on a standard 4G connection.
- **AI Latency**: < 15s for full `JOB_FIT_ANALYSIS` and < 10s for cover letter drafting.
- **Bundle Size**: Maintain V8 bundle size under 300KB (gzipped).
- **Scrape Reliability**: > 95% success rate for supported job portals.

## Quality
- **High-Fidelity Generation**: Cover letters that are role-specific, accurate, and deeply grounded in the user’s unique resume context.
- **Type Safety**: 100% `any`-free production codebase.
- **Error Handling**: 100% coverage for AI-related failure states with clear, actionable errors and graceful user-facing rollbacks.
- **Match Accuracy**: 90% user satisfaction rating on AI-generated fit reasoning.

## Outcomes
- **Generation-to-Application**: Target 75% conversion rate from a "tailored" resume to an actual submission.
- **Interview Conversion**: Track and optimize the "Interview" success rate for analyzed jobs.
