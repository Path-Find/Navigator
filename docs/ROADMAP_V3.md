# Navigator V3 (Future Roadmap)

This document lists future ideas. It is not a committed plan and may change.

## Goal
A Jobs-first product that takes users from finding jobs to outcomes with less manual work.

## Focus areas

### 1. Jobs workspace
- One place for job analysis, resume tailoring, cover letters, interview prep, and outcomes.
- Auto-find jobs from email alerts and the browser extension; allow manual paste.
- Track outcomes: applied -> interview -> offer, with reminders.

### 2. Quality and reliability
- Cover letters that are role-specific, accurate, and grounded in the user’s resume.
- Clear errors and consistent failure handling for AI actions.
- Faster load time target under 2 seconds.

### 3. Pricing and packaging
- Lock actions, not insights.
- Optional packs for Jobs, Coach, and Edu on top of the main subscription.
- Referral credits and short-term access offers.

### 4. Career/Edu (optional)
- Hidden by default; only show when it helps job outcomes.
- Re-enable for internships, career pivots, or education-driven roles.

## Not doing yet
- Standalone Education features that do not help job outcomes.
- Career features that are not tied to a target role.
